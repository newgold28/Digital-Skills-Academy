About DigiSkills Academy

We started with one mission: give every Nigerian the tools to thrive in the digital economy — regardless of background.

Our Story

Bridging the Digital Skills Gap in Nigeria

DigiSkills Academy was founded in 2020 by a group of tech professionals who saw a growing gap between what Nigerian graduates knew and what the job market actually needed.

Since then, we've trained over 2,400 students across web development, design, data, and digital marketing — helping them land remote jobs, freelance clients, and even launch their own agencies. We teach from real experience, not textbooks.

Our programs are designed to be accessible — whether you're a fresh graduate, a career changer, or a business owner who wants to understand the digital tools shaping the world.
